# Role Definition: Admin

**Admins** are the operational managers of the workspace. They handle the day-to-day organization of teams and projects but do not handle billing or workspace deletion.

## 🛠️ Core Permissions
* **Project Oversight:** Create new projects, archive old ones, and define Project Prefixes (e.g., "WEB", "IOS").
* **Team Growth:** Invite new users via email and manage pending invitations.
* **User Lifecycle:** Deactivate or remove Members and Viewers from the organization.
* **Global Issue Management:** Can edit or move any issue within any project, regardless of assignment.

## 🖥️ Unique UI Elements
* **Invite Portal:** Access to the "Invite Team" button and invitation tracking table.
* **Project Configuration:** Sidebar access to modify Project metadata and workflows.
* **User Management Table:** Ability to toggle roles between Member and Viewer.

## 🚫 Restrictions
* Cannot access Billing information.
* Cannot delete the Workspace.
* Cannot remove or demote the Workspace Owner.
