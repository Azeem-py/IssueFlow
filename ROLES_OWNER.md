# Role Definition: Workspace Owner

The **Owner** is the legal and administrative head of the Organization. This role is automatically assigned to the creator of the workspace and holds "Superuser" status.

## 🗝️ Core Permissions
* **Organization Control:** Rename the workspace, update the URL slug, or delete the entire organization.
* **Role Management:** Can promote any Member to Admin or transfer Ownership to another user.
* **Financial Oversight:** Exclusive access to billing, subscription tiers, and payment history.
* **Security Settings:** Configure organization-wide security policies (e.g., forcing SSO or MFA).

## 🖥️ Exclusive UI Elements
* **Organization Settings:** Access to the "Danger Zone" in settings (Delete Workspace).
* **Billing Dashboard:** A dedicated view for invoices and plan management.
* **Audit Logs:** A high-level view of all administrative changes made across the team.

## 🚫 Restrictions
* There can be only one primary Owner at a time (though they can designate multiple Admins).
